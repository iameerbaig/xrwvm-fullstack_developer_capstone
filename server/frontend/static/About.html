About Us

Best Cars Dealership has connected drivers with trusted dealerships since 2015. We operate a nationwide network of over 50 dealerships across 20 states, offering new and pre-owned vehicles from manufacturers including Toyota, Nissan, Audi, Mercedes-Benz, and Kia. Every dealership on our platform is independently reviewed by verified customers, and each review is analysed for sentiment so buyers can see at a glance how a dealership is regarded. Our mission is to make car buying transparent, informed, and fair.
